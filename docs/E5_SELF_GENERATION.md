## Rule (pre-registered)

Plan section 7, E5: with the candidate tape on, sampling at temperatures 0.5-0.8 must not be worse than without it (the model trained without the count heads) on the repeated 4-gram rate, the loop rate and the share copied verbatim; otherwise the self-evidence is down-weighted or the tape switched off at decoding. Operationally: a setting is *not worse* when every paired difference (same prompts, same random numbers) is at most max(2 x paired SE, 0.005). Baseline: the E2 `no_tape` arm when it exists, else the reference with the tape switched off at evaluation time.

Settings: `tape_off` (the tape switched off at evaluation time); `mix_w1` (the tape on, the whole output law tempered, `softmax(log p / T)`: the earlier sampler, self-evidence 1); `net_w<w>` (the tape on, the temperature on the network's distribution only, `p_T = (softmax(z / T) + masses) / (1 + M)`, as the decoder samples, with the masses of candidates whose source is the turn being generated scaled by w: `make_consts(cfg, src_5=w)`; web prompts have no such turn, so only w = 1 is run).

Definitions (`bes/eval/probes.py`): **rep4** = share of the 4-gram occurrences of a sample that repeat an earlier 4-gram of the same sample; **loop** = the sample did not emit END within its budget and its last max(2p, 32) tokens are exactly periodic with a period p <= 32 (an absorbing repetition loop); **copy** = share of generated tokens inside a run of >= 8 tokens that occurs verbatim in the prompt.
